Zhenhui Peng is currently an Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Sun Yat-sen University. He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, under the supervision of Prof. Xiaojuan Ma. He also had a wonderful short-term postdoctoral experience with Prof. Antti Oulasvirta at Aalto University. He received his bachelor's degree at ESE from Nanjing University.

I will join CMA at HKUST(GZ) as a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Spring 2027. I am recruiting Ph.D. students interested in HCI starting in Fall 2027, and I also welcome research interns to join my group starting now (inquiry email should contain a CV and an initial idea). Prospective Ph.D. students are expected to meet at least one of the following criteria: (1) strong recommendation letters from established HCI researchers, (2) a strong publication record, or (3) demonstrated research ability, commitment, and integrity through an internship with my group.

My research focuses on Human-Computer Interaction, Social Computing, and Large Language Models. Specifically, my HaiA (Human-AI Augmentation) group develops computational models of human knowledge and skills, quantitatively analyzes complex human interactions online, and designs effective user interfaces to enhance user performance in higher-order thinking tasks. Our research spans a wide range of application domains, including art and design, learning and education, mental health, social media, clinical medicine, and tourism.

Generally, our work:

  • Understanding People: e.g., what makes social support effective? What knowledge is expressed in comments? What skills are demonstrated in videos?
  • Facilitating Users: e.g., coping with mental health concerns, conversational search in online communities, preparing lesson plans, and ideating creative designs.
  • Teaching Users: e.g., second-language learning, visual design, and critical thinking.
